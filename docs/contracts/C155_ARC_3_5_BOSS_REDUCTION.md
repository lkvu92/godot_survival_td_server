# C155 — Arc 3–5 boss stat reduction

- Version: `1.0.0`
- Status: `ACTIVE`
- Updated: `2026-10-21`

## Rule

For every authored campaign boss in Arc 3, Arc 4, and Arc 5, the spawn HP, ATK, and DEF are reduced by 30% from the prior stage definition. The result is encoded by multiplying each relevant stage boss modifier by `0.70`.

Movement speed, attack speed, attack range, boss skill cadence, status effects, rewards, and wave EXP remain unchanged. Difficulty modifiers continue to apply after the stage modifiers.

## Affected bosses

- Arc 3: Mộc Thần Rừng Già and Thủy Quái Ba Đầu.
- Arc 4: Thiết Hạm Mặt Đất and Chúa Tể Cơ Khí.
- Arc 5: Titan Đất Mẹ and Thần Nguyên Thủy.

## Changelog

- `1.0.0` (2026-10-21): Reduced authored boss HP, ATK, and DEF by 30% for Arc 3–5.
