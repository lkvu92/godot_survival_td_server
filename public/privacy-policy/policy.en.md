# Privacy Policy — Thông Thiên Lộ

**Last updated: 27 October 2026 · Version 1.0**

This policy explains how data is handled when you use **Thông Thiên Lộ**, including the Android application `vn.lkvu92.survivaltd`, its online game services and related support. “We” means the operator of Thông Thiên Lộ. Privacy contact: **admin@lkvustudio.com**.

## 1. Data we process

Depending on the sign-in method and features you use, we and the relevant service providers process these categories:

| Category | Examples and source |
|---|---|
| Account and authentication | Internal user ID, username/display name, email and authentication information for email registration. Google sign-in/linking uses identifiers, email and authentication information provided by Google. Device sign-in uses a device identifier or an app-generated identifier. |
| Gameplay | Characters, selected server, levels, equipment, inventory, currencies, achievements, battle results, rankings, reward history, in-game transactions and related account changes. |
| Purchases and entitlements | Product/order/transaction identifiers, purchase tokens or receipts, purchase/refund time and status, VIP and no-ads entitlements. Google Play processes payments; the game does not receive full payment-card numbers or card security codes through Google Play purchases. |
| Technical and security information | IP address, connection times/status, application version, operating system/device information, error/technical logs, and information needed to detect unauthorized access, fraud or duplicate rewards. |
| Advertising and privacy choices | Google AdMob/Mobile Ads and UMP may process the Android advertising ID or other app/device identifiers, IP address, app/ad interactions, diagnostics and consent choices. IP addresses can indicate approximate location. The game server processes ad-session IDs, internal account IDs, verification transaction IDs and completion status to deliver rewards to the correct account. |
| Support | Email messages, account identifiers and information you voluntarily provide for assistance, account recovery or data-rights requests. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, card details or purchase tokens in support emails. |

The Android build reviewed for this policy does not request access to contacts, microphone, camera or precise GPS location. This does not exclude approximate location derived from IP addresses by advertising providers.

## 2. Purposes

We process data to create and authenticate accounts; save, restore and synchronize progress; provide game features and leaderboards; verify purchases, refunds and entitlements; deliver advertising and verify ad rewards; provide support; maintain stability, security and fair play; resolve disputes; and meet applicable legal obligations.

Not every category is necessary for every feature. You can choose not to use Google sign-in, purchases or rewarded ads. Account, connection and progress data needed for online features may still be processed. Refusing necessary information or requesting its deletion can prevent those features from working.

## 3. Advertising and consent

The game integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on your region, choices and applicable requirements, you may receive consent messages and advertising-related choices. Continuing to use the game is not treated as consent for every advertising purpose where a separate choice is required.

When required by Google, the game's **Privacy Options** entry lets you review or change relevant choices. You can also manage advertising identifiers and advertising choices through Android or Google account settings; the controls vary by device/version. Withdrawal does not automatically delete game data or undo processing lawfully performed before withdrawal. Non-personalized or limited ads can still require data for delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention.

Rewarded ads are optional ways to receive the corresponding benefit. Completion must be verified before rewards are granted. A no-ads entitlement can allow certain rewards without playing a video under game rules; it does not mean that every SDK activity or all technical data processing stops.

See Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [How Google uses data from partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 4. Recipients and disclosure

Data may be processed by hosting, server and operational-support providers for the game; Google when you use Google Sign-In, Google Play Billing, AdMob or UMP; and competent authorities or other parties where necessary for legal requirements, disputes, fraud prevention or protection of rights and safety.

Google and advertising partners process information under their applicable terms and privacy choices. Advertising disclosures can qualify as “sharing” under some privacy rules. We do not provide game-account passwords to advertisers.

Your display name, selected in-game avatar, score/rank or some character information can be visible to other players through leaderboards and public features. Do not put email addresses, phone numbers or sensitive details in display names. Sign-in email is not used as a public leaderboard field.

## 5. Device storage, security and international processing

The app stores some data locally, such as sign-in session tokens, audio/display preferences, language or temporary data. Google SDKs may store consent choices and data needed for SDK operation. In apps, these mechanisms can serve purposes similar to browser cookies.

Production server connections use HTTPS/TLS. We use authentication and access controls appropriate to operating the service. No transmission or storage method is completely secure. Protect your device, email/Google account and sign-in information.

Servers and service providers may process data outside your country. Where applicable rules require it, appropriate protections must be used for international transfers. You may contact us about the relevant protections and rights.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Account and progress data is retained as needed to provide, restore and support maintained accounts. Support and technical records are kept according to the needs of handling requests, troubleshooting, security and operations. Not all technical data is considered necessary to retain indefinitely.

After a deletion request is verified, account and related personal data no longer needed will be deleted or de-identified. Limited transaction, payment/refund, fraud-prevention, dispute or legally required records may be retained for as long as the relevant need continues. If an exception applies to your request, we will explain the categories and reason in our response. Backup copies may remain until replaced or processed through the applicable operational backup cycle and data-protection requirements.

Uninstalling the app, clearing device data, signing out or revoking Google access does not automatically delete server-side account data. The specific request timeline and any retention exceptions will be communicated following verification, in accordance with applicable requirements.

## 7. Your requests and account deletion

Contact **admin@lkvustudio.com** to request access, correction, a copy, deletion, objection/restriction or withdrawal of consent, to the extent those rights apply to you. You may also complain to the relevant data-protection authority where you live.

To request deletion, email **“Thông Thiên Lộ — Account deletion request”**, using the linked email if available, and include your account ID and server/character name if known. If you use device sign-in or cannot access the linked email, explain the situation so we can provide a suitable verification route. We ask only for information needed to protect the account and verify the request, not passwords or one-time codes by email.

Deletion can remove progress, characters, inventory and access to account-linked content. Google-controlled records, such as Google Play payment history, follow Google's separate processes. Deletion does not automatically change refund rights or obligations under platform terms.

See the [account-deletion instructions](../delete-account/index.html). You do not need the game installed to email a request.

## 8. Younger players

Refer to the age group and use restrictions published on the game's Google Play listing. If you cannot independently give the necessary consent under the rules where you live, ask a parent or guardian for help before providing optional data. Parents or guardians can contact us if they believe a child's data has been handled improperly or want us to review and delete relevant information.

## 9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when features, providers or applicable requirements change. The update date appears at the top. For material changes to processing purposes or choices requiring consent, we will provide appropriate notice or request a choice rather than relying only on a changed date.
